Getting your data in
From Apple Health (runs, sleep, steps, heart rate and more)
  1. Open the Health app → tap your profile picture → Export All Health Data → Export.
  2. Choose Save to Files, then tap Import at the top of this app and pick export.zip.
From Strava (on strava.com in Safari, not the Strava app)
  1. One run: open the activity → ⋯ menu → Export GPX or Export Original. Import the downloaded file.
  2. Everything: profile picture → Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request Your Archive. Strava emails you a link; download the zip and import it. Only runs are taken from it.
Runs found in both Health and Strava are merged, not doubled. Everything is read on your phone; nothing is uploaded.
