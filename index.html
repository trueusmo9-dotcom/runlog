How do I get my data out of the watch?
  1. In the CMF Watch app, make sure Apple Health sync is on and your runs have synced.
  2. Open the Health app → tap your profile picture → Export All Health Data → Export.
  3. When the share sheet appears, choose Save to Files.
  4. Come back here, tap Import run data, and pick export.zip.
You can also import .gpx files (from Strava, for example), or the unzipped export.xml (no routes in that case). Everything is read on your phone; nothing is uploaded.
